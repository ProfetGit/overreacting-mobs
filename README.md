**One hit, big drama.** Mobs react to every hit with weighty, cartoony animations: they freeze in a white flash, fly back with their arms flung out, land in a stumble and shake it off. Kills get their own dramatic deaths. Client-side only, for Fabric, NeoForge and Forge on Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3.

![Features](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/overreacting-mobs/main/docs/desc/title-features.png)

- **Hit reactions** for front, side and back hits, each with an impact freeze, a flight and a landing. Crits and sprint hits send mobs flying further.
- **Combos**: keep hitting the same mob and the reactions build up, ending in a heavy knockout blow. A weak, uncharged swing gets a small flinch.
- **Faces**: mobs squint on impact, go wide-eyed in the air and see stars after a big hit.
- **Deaths**: the killing blow plays a death to match the hit. The body stays for a moment, then poofs. Fire, falls and drowning get a collapse.
- **Built per body type**, so a zombie, a cow, a spider and a creeper each move in their own way. Babies included.
- **Visual only**: damage, knockback and hitboxes stay exactly as in vanilla.

![Supported mobs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/overreacting-mobs/main/docs/desc/title-supported-mobs.png)

- **Zombies and skeletons**: zombie, husk, drowned, zombie villager, zombified piglin, skeleton, stray, bogged, parched, wither skeleton, piglin, piglin brute
- **Villagers and illagers**: villager, wandering trader, witch, vindicator, pillager, evoker, illusioner
- **Animals**: cow, mooshroom, pig, sheep, goat, panda, polar bear
- **Pets**: wolf, fox, cat, ocelot
- **Others**: spider, cave spider, creeper, enderman, iron golem
- The babies of all of these

Other mobs keep their vanilla animations. More body types are on the way.

![Compatibility](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/overreacting-mobs/main/docs/desc/title-compatibility.png)

- **Client-side only.** Works on any server, including vanilla servers and Realms. Only you see the animations.
- Works with **Fresh Animations** (EMF + ETF): the reactions play on top of its models.

![Installation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/overreacting-mobs/main/docs/desc/title-installation.png)

Put the jar for your loader and Minecraft version in your `mods` folder:
- **Fabric** (no Fabric API needed)
- **NeoForge**
- **Forge**

To uninstall, remove the jar. Nothing is saved to your worlds.

![Good to know](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ProfetGit/overreacting-mobs/main/docs/desc/title-good-to-know.png)

- The animations only change what you see. Where a mob really is, and where it can hit you from, is unchanged.
- Players without the mod see vanilla hits.
